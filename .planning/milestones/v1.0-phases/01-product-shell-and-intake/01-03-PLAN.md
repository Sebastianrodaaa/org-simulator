**Threats:**
- **Medium:** Right panel remains empty or business-oriented, making the new product feel like a text form rather than a simulator.

**Mitigations:**
- Add static preview using advisor-perspective nodes.
- Rename graph/header/legend/count labels to advisor/risk language.
- Verify forbidden terms are absent from primary flow.

</threat_model>
