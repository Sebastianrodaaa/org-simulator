<objective>
Convert the left side of `/simulate` into the CivicBridge track, guided scenario, structured fact, uncertainty chip, and safe run-intake experience.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Threat Model

**Assets:** Sensitive user context, legal-safety boundaries, demo trust.

**Threats:**
- **High:** Intake feels like legal advice or encourages users to enter private documents/secrets.
- **Medium:** Old business demo scenario remains visible and undermines product trust.
- **Medium:** Empty/unknown facts are treated as errors instead of uncertainty.

**Mitigations:**
- Add visible planning-aid/not-legal-advice safety note before run.
- Replace CFO/budget demo with founder and first-1000-days scenarios.
- Allow empty fields and uncertainty chips; do not block run on missing key facts if situation text exists.

</threat_model>
